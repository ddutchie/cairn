# Task Force — Game Design & World Vision

## What It Is
**Task Force** is a crisp vertical-scrolling stacked-voxel arcade shooter seeded from your Cairn workspace. Each project becomes a themed sortie (`PROJECT 1 OF N`) over scrolling procedural islands, with your project's tasks flying in as enemy squadrons and a project-deadline boss at the end.

## What We're Trying to Achieve
- **Benchmark Arcade Pacing:** Immediate, tactile action — banking left/right, firing twin-linked bolts, chain-exploding waves, screen shake, and drifting clouds with ground shadows.
- **Workspace Integration:** Task priorities map to enemy hulls/colors (`urgent`, `high`, `medium`, `low`), and clearing a project ships it for a bonus life.
