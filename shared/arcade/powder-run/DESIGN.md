# Powder Run — Game Design & World Vision

## What It Is
**Powder Run** is a fast-paced 3D halfpipe snowboarding arcade game powered by `shared/arcade/pixel/raster3d.ts`. Each Cairn project becomes a themed alpine mountain run (`PROJECT 1 OF N`) where you carve down a sweeping 3D halfpipe, collect your project's **Tasks** and **Notes**, launch off the pipe lips, and click/tap in midair to throw random snowboard tricks.

## What We're Trying to Achieve
- **Built for the 3D Engine:** A U-shaped 3D halfpipe curving away toward the horizon gives immediate speed, clear spatial readability, and dramatic vertical launches.
- **Task Force-Style Arcade Loop:** Instant action, project-by-project runs, priority-colored task collectibles, high-lip note pickups, and overdue-task boulder hazards you can carve around or soar over.
- **Air Tricks & Combo Flow:** Clicking, tapping, or pressing `Space` while airborne triggers a random trick (`360 SPIN`, `BACKFLIP`, `METHOD GRAB`, `INDY`, `RODEO`, `NOSE GRAB`) that animates your 3D voxel boarder and builds a combo multiplier on landing.
