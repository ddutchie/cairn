## What's new in Cairn 3.2.4

### Changed

#### Sync

- **More groundwork for People.** Cairn can now read who attended a meeting note and when it happened from optional `attendees` and `date` properties, and keep those properties up to date. This stays switched off until the People feature launches, so nothing changes on screen yet, and existing `attendees` properties in your vault are left alone.
- A conflicted copy of a note no longer stays linked to the original note's meeting, so a meeting won't list the same note twice.
