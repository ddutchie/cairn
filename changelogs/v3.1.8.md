## What's new in Cairn 3.1.8

### Fixed

- **Linux downloads are about half the size.** The Linux AppImages bundled NVIDIA GPU libraries (CUDA and TensorRT, ~350 MB) that Cairn never uses, plus the other architecture's embedding runtime. They are now stripped from the package, bringing each AppImage from ~425 MB down to roughly 190 MB.

### Removed

- **The unreleased Plugins preview is gone.** Settings → Extensions no longer shows a Plugins tab, and the developer-only plugin loader (`CAIRN_PLUGINS_DEV`) has been removed. Tools, Commands and MCP servers are unaffected.
