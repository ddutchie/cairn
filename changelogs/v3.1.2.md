## What's new in Cairn 3.1.2

### Added

- **Images in your notes now sync to your phone.** Pictures you paste or embed (`![[photo.png]]`) travel through the same sync folder as your notes, so they appear on your other devices instead of a broken link. Images added from the phone arrive the same way. Existing images are picked up automatically on the next sync. Your own files are never overwritten: if two devices have a different image with the same name, both are kept.

### Changed

- Device Sync protocol is now version 4. An older Cairn on another device will still sync notes and tasks, but won't send or receive images until it's updated, and shows its "needs updating" hint.

### Fixed

- **MCP tool token estimates are now accurate.** The per-tool and per-server token badges in Settings → Tools previously counted only the name and description, missing the input schema (often most of the cost), so large tools showed a fraction of their real size. They now measure the full definition sent to the model.
