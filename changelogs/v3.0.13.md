## What's new in Cairn 3.0.13

### Improved

- **Much lower memory use in large workspaces.** The app no longer keeps the full text of every note in memory. It keeps each note's title, preview and details, and loads a note's text when you open it or when something needs it, such as templates, AI context or backlinks. The most recently used notes stay cached, so switching between them is still instant. At 10,000 notes this cuts the memory the app holds for notes from about 40 MB to about 7 MB, whatever the size of your notes. Search, backlinks and Export data still cover the full text of every note.
