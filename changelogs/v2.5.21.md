## Fixes

- **Per-request cost shown for NeuralWatt providers.** NeuralWatt reports cost as a top-level `cost.request_cost_usd` field (not inside `usage`) on non-streaming responses, and omits it entirely from streaming. The chat loop now reads the top-level field as a fallback, and when no cost is reported inline, diffs the `/quota` endpoint before/after the stream to recover the per-request cost. Sub-cent costs display as `<$0.01`.
