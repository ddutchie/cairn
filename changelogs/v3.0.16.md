## What's new in Cairn 3.0.16

### Fixed

- **Note and task cards are back in chat.** When the assistant read or edited a note or task, the chat showed a plain "Used get_note" / "Updated note …" chip instead of the clickable card with the note's title. Live tool results were being read in the old session format and came through empty, so no card could be built. Results are now read correctly, and the card also survives very long notes, whose output is cut off before it can be parsed.
