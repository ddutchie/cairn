## What's new

### Fixed

- **Deleting an automation now removes its folder from disk.** Previously the automation's row (and run history) were deleted from the database, but its on-disk folder (`<project>/.automations/<id>/` — scripts, `out/`, `.env`, manifest, and every run's transcript + scratch) was left behind. Deleting an automation now also removes the folder and purges its keychain env secrets, so nothing orphaned lingers after the automation is gone.
