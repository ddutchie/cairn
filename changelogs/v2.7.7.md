## What's new

### Fixed

- **Mermaid nodes with light fills are readable again in dark mode.** Diagrams that pin their own fill colours (LLM-generated charts often pick light pastels) rendered white text on a light background, because mermaid's label colour follows the theme. The contrast pass that already fixed subgraph labels now covers regular nodes too — it measures each node's fill and flips the label to a contrasting dark or light colour unless the diagram author set a label colour of their own.
