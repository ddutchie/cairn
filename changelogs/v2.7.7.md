## What's new

### Changed

- **The coding agent now runs on the new Cordis engine by default.** The agent that edits your files, runs commands, and works through plans has been ported onto Cairn's next-generation runtime, matching the chat engine. Everything you already rely on carries over — plan mode, per-tool approvals, the repeated-action safety pause, skills, image attachments, and automatic context compaction — with resumable sessions and cleaner streaming. The previous engine remains available as a fallback (set `CAIRN_ENGINE=builtin`) while the new one settles in.

### Fixed

- **Mermaid nodes with light fills are readable again in dark mode.** Diagrams that pin their own fill colours (LLM-generated charts often pick light pastels) rendered white text on a light background, because mermaid's label colour follows the theme. The contrast pass that already fixed subgraph labels now covers regular nodes too — it measures each node's fill and flips the label to a contrasting dark or light colour unless the diagram author set a label colour of their own.
