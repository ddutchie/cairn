## What's new in Cairn 3.1.0

### New

- **Shipped this week and At risk on the project Overview.** Cards now record when they were finished (when they enter a Done column; cleared if moved back out, kept when archived). The Overview shows everything finished in the last 7 days, and a list of open cards that need attention: overdue, blocked by an open card while due within 3 days, or sitting in In Progress / Review with no updates for a week. Existing Done cards are backfilled from their last update time.
- **Weekly review and daily brief notes.** A new button on the Overview header writes a review note into the project's `Reviews` folder: what shipped, what's at risk, the next unblocked cards by priority, and wikilinks to every note touched in the period, with a space for your own reflections. It's plain markdown, so you can edit it or ask the agent to expand on it.
