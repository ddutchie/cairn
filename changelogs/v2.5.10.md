# v2.5.10

## Added

- **See your provider's remaining credits.** When a saved AI provider reports a credit balance (for example OpenRouter, via its key-info endpoint), Settings → AI and the Coding Agent settings now show a small "credits left" badge under the model picker — the remaining balance, plus the amount used and your limit on hover. It's fetched alongside the model list (and refreshed whenever you Refresh models), stays out of the way for providers that don't expose credits, and never blocks the UI if the lookup fails. Your key is resolved in the background process as usual, so it's never handled by the in-app browser layer.

## Fixed

- **"Lines changed since you last viewed this note" banner no longer vanishes instantly.** When an agent, the MCP server, or device sync edited a note, the changed-lines banner and highlights would flash and disappear the moment you opened the note. Clearing the one-shot change mark was re-triggering the effect that draws the highlight and immediately wiping it. The banner and highlights now stay put until you dismiss them (✕), start editing, or navigate away — the 6.5s auto-timeout that could hide changes before you'd read them has been removed.
- **Settings no longer crashes with an infinite render loop.** Opening Settings → AI (or Coding Agent) could throw "The result of getSnapshot should be cached to avoid an infinite loop." The saved-providers selector was building a fresh `setModel` function on every render, which defeated `useShallow`'s snapshot caching. The wrapper is now created outside the selector against the stable store actions.
