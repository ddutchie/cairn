# v2.5.10

## Added

- **See your provider's remaining credits.** When a saved AI provider reports a credit balance (for example OpenRouter, via its key-info endpoint), Settings → AI and the Coding Agent settings now show a small "credits left" badge under the model picker — the remaining balance, plus the amount used and your limit on hover. It's fetched alongside the model list (and refreshed whenever you Refresh models), stays out of the way for providers that don't expose credits, and never blocks the UI if the lookup fails. Your key is resolved in the background process as usual, so it's never handled by the in-app browser layer.
