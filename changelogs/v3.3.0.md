## What's new in Cairn 3.3.0

### Added

#### People

- **People is part of Cairn.** It no longer sits behind a preview switch: the People view is in the sidebar for everyone (⌘7), the `people` tool, `@` mentions in chat, meeting attendees and owners on tasks are on, and Today's 1:1 prep and nudges work out of the box. Hide the view any time in Settings → Sidebar & views. If you hid People while it was a preview, it appears once and stays however you set it after that.

- **People pictures in the People view.** A switcher at the top of People adds Org chart and Relationships (moved over from Insights, which keeps them too), plus two new ones: **Teams** groups everyone by team, and **Time zones** lays out each person's working day against yours in a 24-hour strip, with who is at work or asleep right now. Click anyone to open them in a side panel.
- **Rhythm, Bridges and Map.** Three more People pictures. **Rhythm** shows who you are due to see, against the cadence you set. **Bridges** shows the people you reach others through, and who would drop off your map without them. **Map** puts everyone on a world map from their time zone, shades the night, and marks who is probably offline; a slider shows who is online later or earlier.

### Fixed

#### People

- **Meeting notes you already had are picked up.** Notes whose `attendees` property was on disk before People read it (imported or edited while People was off) now have their attendees captured once at startup, instead of waiting for the file to change.
- **Add person works from every People view,** the time zone view keeps the minutes of someone's working hours (3:30, not 3:00), and Relationships and Org chart no longer show blank when People isn't available.
- **Opening a person from elsewhere works first time.** Clicking a person in Today, or "Open person" on a chat tool card, used to land on the People list without opening them unless you tried again. People now keeps the person you were sent to.
- **The "scan notes for people" tool call has a plain name.** It read "Used People"; it now reads "Scanned notes for people".
- **Tool cards stay open while the assistant is still working.** Opening a tool card during a running turn could collapse it a moment later. Cairn tool cards, connector cards such as Slack and the plain tool chips (bash, read, edit) now all stay open.

#### Chat and People

- **The context ring shows the real context size.** A long chat that reloaded from disk could read far over 100% (a 137K context showed as 2.7M of 1M), because it added up every model request in the session. It now reads the latest request, and the prompt cache figures match.
- **Slack profiles fill in.** People's title, pronouns and time zone from Slack never appeared with Slack's own connector, because its reply (labelled text) wasn't recognised. It is now, and profiles that failed before refresh the next time you open the person.
- **Slack tells you when it can't read a profile.** The person page says "Slack profile unavailable" with the reason, and the Slack id field in Edit shows whether their profile was read and when. Status emoji from Slack show as emoji, not `:headphones:`. Every standard emoji name is recognised; a custom one (`:partyparrot:`) shows only the status text.

#### People design pass

- **The person page fits the side panel.** The name is smaller beside the pictures, and the Edit fields stack under their labels instead of squeezing the inputs. The two "Relationship" fields are now "Relationship to you" and "Links to others", and the buttons read "Edit details" and "Edit persona" so you can tell them apart.
- **One layout across the People pictures.** Teams, Time zones, Rhythm, Bridges and Map share the same page, heading and spacing. The Time zones strip shrinks to fit beside the side panel instead of scrolling sideways, and says "6h shared" or "no shared hours" in words. Rhythm explains its bar, line and dot.
- **People search and filter moved into the list.** They sat in the toolbar and pushed the buttons onto a second row. "Find in notes" is now "Find people in notes".

#### Relationships map

- **The relationships map now uses the Knowledge Graph's canvas.** People and their links are drawn by the same engine as the Knowledge Graph, so the map settles off the main thread, remembers where people sat when you come back, and zooms and labels the same way. Link kinds keep their colours and the chips still filter them; former links stay dashed, "meetings together" lines are thicker for more meetings, and you are drawn larger in the accent colour. Hover a line for its description; click a person to open them and light up who they are linked to.
- **Each person shows their connections.** A person's page, and the side panel, has a Connections picture: they sit in the middle and the people they are linked to (manager, reports, mentor, stakeholders, peers) are drawn around them, coloured by kind. Click a neighbour to go to them. "Their links" adds one more step out, and "Meetings together" adds people they meet with. A person with no links shows nothing.
- **A location fills in the time zone, and puts people on the map in their own city.** Setting a person's location to a city (San Francisco, Montreal, Bengaluru, from a list of about 7,000) fills an empty time zone. You can also type a city into the Time zone box and it is stored as its zone (Montreal is America/Toronto; there is no Montreal zone). The Map pins someone at their own city when it lies in their time zone, instead of the zone's principal city. Autofill from a persona note proposes the zone with the location. A time zone you set yourself is never replaced.
- **Review fixes.** A single Slack "Status:" line is no longer taken for a whole profile. Connections labels stay visible in the small graph, a person focused from the keyboard is highlighted on the canvas, directed links read "Ann manages Bo" in the hover text, and the Time zones hour labels thin out when the side panel is open.
