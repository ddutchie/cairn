## What's new in Cairn 3.2.5

### Fixed

#### People

- **`[[Wikilinks]]` in a person's persona note now work on their page.** They show as links that open the matching note, and as a muted link when no note has that title, the same as in the note editor.
- **Mark yourself as "me" in People.** Editing a person now has a "This is me" switch. Relationships like direct report, manager, peer or mentor link a person to you on the Relationships map, which only works once someone is marked as you; before, there was no way to do that, so those relationships never showed. The map now says so when it's the reason links are missing.
- **A person opens in a side panel.** Clicking someone on the Relationships map or the Org chart in Insights now opens their page beside the canvas, with Edit, instead of leaving Insights ("Open in People" is in the panel's header). With the chat at full width, opening a person (from Today's 1:1 nudges, for example) uses the preview pane beside the chat, like notes and tasks, rather than switching away from it.
- **The People tool reads like Cairn's other tools in chat.** A `people` call now shows as a "Cairn · …" card with a plain summary ("Read Ana Lopez", "Linked Alex Morgan (manages)"). Opened, it lists the people a search found with their relationships, shows a person's details, timeline or relationships, and says in words what a save or a link changed. An "Open person" button opens the person.
- **Relationship labels stay tidy.** A relationship to you is one of the fixed types (direct report, manager, peer, stakeholder, mentor, buddy, recruiter, candidate, other) plus an optional short label for more detail, such as "executive sponsor" on a stakeholder. An assistant that put a list like "manager, tech lead" in a label is now told to give each relationship its own entry. You can edit the label under each selected type in a person's settings, and it shows as "Stakeholder · Executive sponsor".
