## What's new

### Fixes
- **A note deleted on another device is no longer offered for restore twice.** When you delete a note on one device and edit that same note on another before it has seen the deletion, Device Sync keeps your edit as a conflict copy — and previously it *also* listed the note as "deleted on another device" with a Restore button. Restoring then left you with two identical notes to clean up. Cairn now recognises that your edit is already safe in the conflict copy and points you to the conflicts view to resolve it, instead of offering a redundant restore. Notes deleted with no competing edit still show Restore as before.
