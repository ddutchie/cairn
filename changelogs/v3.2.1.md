## What's new in Cairn 3.2.1

### Changed

#### Chat

- **Cairn's own tool calls look like connector calls.** Notes, tasks, projects, idea flow, writing style and the rest of Cairn's data tools now show as a "Cairn · action" card with labelled Arguments and Result panels, the same as MCP and HTTP service calls, instead of a bare chip with a raw JSON dump.
- **Tool calls show what changed.** Adding to your writing style shows the added line under its section; task updates list each field as `from → to`; note edits show a diff; idea-flow changes are summarised with the new ideas as chips; searches list their top hits. Writes open automatically and keep the raw call under "Raw call". Failed calls still show the error.
- **Approval cards show the pending change.** For Cairn's own tools the card previews the diff or field changes about to happen rather than truncated JSON.
- Task updates now report each field that changed (old and new value, including column moves and blockers) and note renames report the old and new title; the in-app `update_user_writing_style` tool no longer returns your whole style guide in its reply.

#### Today

- **A clearer Today page.** The headline is larger and set in the display face, and the row of stat boxes is gone: decisions and drafts are already counted beside the stack, and free time and meetings left now sit in the "Your day" header.
- **Decisions come first.** "Need you" gets the wider column with "Prepared for you" and "Moving without you" stacked beside it, the sections (including Your day and Your projects) are open headings rather than boxes around boxes, and the focus decision's title is larger.
- **Shorter day section.** The block-by-block list under the day strip is folded behind "List all blocks" so the decisions stay in view, and "Your day" is now a raised card. Decision and draft cards have a light lift.

#### Overview

- **Overview shares Today's language, with its depth kept.** Sections use the same display-face heading with a rule, numerals are set in the display face, and the developer note under Recent runs is gone. The progress card keeps its glowing ring (the ring shows the percentage, the numeral shows "5 of 43") and the project title keeps its italic suffix. Task flow, Open by priority and Project health stay as raised cards that tilt toward the pointer.
- **A leaner at-a-glance strip.** The four tiles use a status dot instead of a coloured stripe, and the Completion tile now shows what shipped this week rather than repeating the percentage. Needs attention gets its own heading, and its items keep their soft status tint.

#### Design

- **Design language.** `docs/design-language.md` documents how Cairn views are composed (headings, item cards, the hero, status colour, copy) so new work starts from the same place. It is linked from `AGENTS.md`.
