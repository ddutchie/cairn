## What's new in Cairn 3.0.15

### Fixed

- **Older chats open again after the 3.0.14 upgrade.** Some saved chats could not be upgraded to the new session format and showed up empty, because the upgrade rejected a tool result that had been trimmed after its turn ended. These chats now upgrade and open normally.
- **No more flood of old notifications after an update or restart.** Every unread notification (such as "Patch applied to …") was shown as a system notification again each time the app started. Only notifications that arrive while the app is running are shown now.
- **No more stray "New chat" entries below your chats.** Each chat also created a hidden duplicate entry under a different id, which appeared as an empty "New chat" in the conversation list. New chats no longer do this, and empty duplicates are cleaned up when a workspace loads. A chat's title is also no longer reset when it is re-indexed.
- **A chat that fails to load is no longer treated as empty.** If a chat's history cannot be read, its entry is now kept instead of being pruned as a blank thread, and the failure is logged.
