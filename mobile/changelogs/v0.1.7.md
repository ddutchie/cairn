## What's new

### Changed

- **The "device needs updating" hint now explains what an update gets you.** The Sync screen previously said updating fixes deletions — which is true for older sync versions but not for the current one, where the real gain is your **writing style**. When a paired device is one version behind, it now says "Update Cairn there so your writing style syncs to this device" instead of the deletion wording.

- **The Sync screen shows your writing-style status.** A row tells you whether your writing style has synced from the desktop (with its last-updated date, and your name if one's set) or not yet — so you know at a glance whether this device can draft in your voice.
