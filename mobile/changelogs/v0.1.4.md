## What's new

A guided first-run setup that walks you through connecting your phone to your desktop.

### New

- **Guided sync setup on first launch**: The first time you open Cairn (before any workspace is connected), you now get a step-by-step guide instead of a bare "connect a workspace" screen. It makes the non-obvious ordering clear: opening the app is what *creates* the shared iCloud folder (`iCloud Drive › Cairn › sync`) — which the guide confirms live — then tells you exactly where to go on your computer (**Settings › Device Sync › Choose folder…**) and to point it at that same folder. The guide then watches iCloud on its own and, the moment your desktop publishes a workspace, shows a **Connect** button you tap to open it — no manual refreshing needed. It also detects when iCloud isn't signed in and tells you how to fix it. This replaces the old static four-step list (which listed the steps in the wrong order and gave no live feedback about whether anything was working).
- **Archive or delete a task by dragging it**: Long-press a card on the board to lift it and an **Archive** and **Delete** drop zone slide up at the bottom — drag the card onto one to archive it or delete it, mirroring the desktop board. The zones light up as you hover them (and the card outline turns amber for archive / red for delete); Delete asks for confirmation first (and the removal syncs to your other devices). You can also **delete a task from its detail screen's ⋯ menu**, alongside Archive. This is the first way to *delete* a task on mobile — previously you could only archive one.
- **Liquid Glass app icon on iOS**: Cairn now ships an Icon Composer app icon, so on iOS 26 the Home Screen icon picks up the system's Liquid Glass treatment — including automatic light, dark, tinted, and clear appearances — instead of a flat image.

### Fixes

- **Semantic search now indexes every note (one bad note no longer stalls the rest)**: The on-device index could get stuck reporting something like "209 of 212 items indexed" and never finish. A single note whose text the embedder couldn't process threw an error that aborted the *entire* indexing pass — so that note **and every note after it** were left unindexed. Each note (and task) is now indexed in isolation: if one can't be embedded it's skipped and the rest carry on, so the index reaches completion. Empty notes (no body text) are also no longer counted in the total, since there's nothing to embed.
- **See why an item isn't indexed**: The "items indexed" readout on the Search tab (and the Sync screen's index card) now has an info button when something isn't fully indexed. Tap it for a plain explanation and the list of notes involved — whether they're empty (nothing to index) or have content that couldn't be embedded (usually a body of only symbols, links, or an image), with guidance to add some text and reindex.
