## What's new

### Added

- **Workspace and Project widgets for the iOS Home Screen.** The Workspace widget shows your overall progress, the day's overdue and due-today tasks, and your project and note counts. The Project widget shows one project's progress with its start and target dates; long-press it and choose **Edit Widget** to pick the project, and add it more than once to keep several projects in view. Both match the Overview's progress card, follow light and dark mode and your accent colour, refresh when the app opens, syncs or goes to the background, and open the workspace or the project when tapped. Small, medium and large sizes are supported.

- **A Quick capture widget and app-icon shortcuts.** The Quick capture widget (Home Screen, or a circular Lock Screen widget) opens the capture sheet in one tap. Long-pressing the Cairn icon now offers **Quick capture** and **Search**.

- **A working indicator that follows what the assistant is doing.** While a reply is in flight the chat shows an animated loader below the message: one for waiting on the model, one for thinking, one for writing the answer and one while a tool runs. The tool chips use the same loader. The loaders are drawn natively, so they appear instantly and stay smooth while a reply streams.

- **Numbers roll and the progress ring sweeps.** The completion percent, the done / total count and the Usage totals count up like an odometer, and the progress ring sweeps to its new value, both from where they were rather than from zero.

- **The empty message box and the notes filter suggest things to try.** The placeholder rotates through example prompts every few seconds, and stops as soon as you tap in, type, or have VoiceOver or Reduce Motion on.

- **Search results fade in row by row, and loading placeholders shimmer** instead of pulsing.

- **A soft glow breathes around the message box while the assistant is working**, slower while it thinks or waits and quicker during a tool call. With Reduce Motion it is a still ring.

- **Reasoning and tool-call panels open with a spring and a light tap**, and the chevron turns with them.

- **Search ranks exact names and ids better and repeats itself less.** Semantic search over notes and tasks now fuses meaning-based and keyword rankings, boosts results that contain every search term, and spreads near-duplicates apart. The assistant's note and task search returns fewer, cleaner results when only a few are clearly relevant.

- **Chat replies render on the native text engine.** Tables scroll sideways instead of squashing, code blocks are syntax-highlighted natively, math draws without a web view, and long replies stream more smoothly. Wikilinks, callouts, synced images, Mermaid diagrams and colour literals (`#ff0000`) keep working.

- **The hidden Breakout and Cairn Stacker games feel livelier.** Scores roll like an odometer and a streak chip pops as it grows. Breakout's ball leaves a glow and a short trail, the paddle squashes when it catches the ball, bricks shrink away as they break, and the haptics step up the longer your streak runs (Breakout now also remembers your best score). In Stacker, stones settle with a slight squash as they land, a perfect landing sends out a ring and a floating "Perfect ×N", the camera glides up as the tower grows, and a missed stone tumbles away with a small screen shake. The end card rises in piece by piece and pops when you set a new best. With Reduce Motion on, the effects are skipped and only the haptics and text remain.

- **Try Cairn without a computer.** Setup now offers **Explore with sample data**: a small "Welcome to Cairn" workspace with notes, tasks and a sample chat that stays on this phone and never syncs. Once your desktop publishes, pick your own workspace from the workspace menu.

- **Cairn asks before sending your data to an AI provider.** The first time chat or a note AI action talks to an OpenAI-compatible service, Cairn says what it sends (your messages and the notes and tasks the assistant reads) and asks you to Allow or Don't allow. It asks once per service. Endpoints on this phone or your local network, and Apple Intelligence, are never asked about.

### Fixed

- **The back button after following a link from chat now says "Back"** instead of "(tabs)".

- **Chat retries a flaky connection.** A busy provider, a rate limit or a dropped connection no longer ends the reply straight away: before anything has streamed, chat now retries a few times with a short wait, as desktop does, and only then shows the connection message. Bad keys, quota and billing errors still fail immediately.

- **Switching apps mid-reply no longer loses the answer.** If iOS cuts the connection while Cairn is in the background, chat quietly restarts the reply when you come back (only when no tool had started, so nothing runs twice). Otherwise the text that had already streamed is kept, with the error note underneath.

- **Unsaved note edits are kept.** Edits you hadn't saved used to vanish if you swiped back or iOS closed Cairn in the background. They're now kept on this phone and reopen in the editor next time, and leaving a note with unsaved changes asks whether to discard them.

- **Edits reach your computer sooner.** A change made just before you switch apps now syncs as Cairn goes to the background, instead of waiting until you next open it, and iOS now lets that sync finish before pausing the app. Cairn also catches up in the background from time to time, so your widgets and the next launch start with your computer's latest edits (iOS decides when, often overnight; it needs Background App Refresh on). Quiet syncs also stop re-uploading an unchanged sync file to iCloud.

- **A crash on one screen no longer blanks the app.** You get a "Something went wrong" screen with Try again and Copy details. If the workspace stored on this phone can't be opened, you can reset it and download it again from iCloud.

- **Updating Cairn can't leave the database half-upgraded.** Each upgrade step now completes fully or not at all, so an update interrupted part-way finishes cleanly on the next launch.

- **Long chats stay smooth.** Only the latest 40 messages are kept on screen; **Show earlier messages** loads more.

- **VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.** Icon-only buttons (clear search, clear due date, remove attachment, close AI actions) now have spoken labels, task checkboxes read as checkboxes with their state, and the sync ring no longer spins with Reduce Motion on.

- **No more Face ID permission text.** Cairn never used Face ID, and the unused permission description is gone.

### Removed

- **The built-in Rork AI endpoint is gone.** Rork is retiring it, so chat and the note AI actions now use Apple Intelligence (where the device supports it) or your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key under Settings → AI. If you were on the built-in endpoint you'll land on the OpenAI-compatible setup: add a base URL and key to keep chatting.

### Changed

- **Dependency updates.** Updated React Native to 0.88.1, Expo to 58.0.22 and the Expo modules to the versions that match it, plus other packages to their latest compatible releases. No change to how Cairn looks or behaves.
- **Newer diagram and math engines.** Mermaid diagrams and KaTeX math in chat and notes are rendered with Mermaid 12 and KaTeX 0.19. Also updated gesture-handler to 3.4.
