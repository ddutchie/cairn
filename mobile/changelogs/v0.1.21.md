## What's new

### Added

- **People is on by default.** The People screens, `@` mentions and the `people` tool no longer need a hidden switch; the Developer switch under Settings → Appearance is gone.

- **@ a person in chat.** Type `@` in the chat to see matching people above the keyboard and tap one; the assistant then gets what Cairn knows about them. Private persona notes stay out.

- **Role, team, location and time zone on a person.** A person's page shows them under the name, and a new Details button edits them along with their Slack id. A time zone is checked before it saves. The People list shows the role under each name.
