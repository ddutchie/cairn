## What's new

### Added

- **People is on by default.** The People screens, `@` mentions and the `people` tool no longer need a hidden switch; the Developer switch under Settings → Appearance is gone. The "People preview" label is retired.
- **Delete a workspace from the phone.** Open the sync screen and swipe a workspace left to delete it. Cairn removes its sync files from iCloud for every device and deletes this phone's copy. If your computer is still connected to the same folder it uploads the workspace again, so deleting also works as a fresh re-sync.
- **Removed on the computer? The phone stops re-uploading it.** When the desktop removes a workspace from the sync folder, the phone pauses syncing it and says so on the sync screen, instead of publishing it back and keeping it alive.
- **Teams sync from the desktop.** A team's details (what it owns, its kind, its lead, the team above it, its channel and links) and who is on it, with their roles and past members, come across with your people. A person's team on their page is their own team; someone can also be in guilds and projects. The assistant's `people` tool can list, update, merge and delete teams here too, merge duplicate people, archive someone and record that a relationship ended.

### Fixed

- **Renaming a team on one device no longer drops people edited on another.** Teams keep their identity through a rename.
- **People the assistant adds become real people.** Adding or updating a person as a stated fact now confirms someone who was only suggested (for example, a meeting attendee name), so they appear in your list.
