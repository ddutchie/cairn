## What's new

### Fixed

- **Toolbar actions no longer pile onto the tab rail in folded-landscape Duo.** When the iPhone Duo is folded in landscape, the vertical side rail only has room for one toolbar pill above the five-tab bar (portrait is tall enough for three). A shared `useTightSideRail` / `railToolbar` helper in `screen-chrome` (plus `ChromeColumn` and `ProjectsTreeSidebar`) now collapses any screen or split-view panel with more than one rail button — Projects, Calendar, Knowledge Graph, Chat, Search, and open notes/tasks — into a single native `•••` overflow menu while keeping all buttons inline in portrait and unfolded layouts.

- **The Chat model name no longer spills over the header.** Long model ids overflowed the centred title slot and painted over the context ring, looking off-centre. The title now caps to the space between the header items and truncates with an ellipsis.

- **Quick Capture's buttons stay reachable while typing.** The input footer (dictate, Paste, Save as note, File it) and the proposal footer (Back, Confirm) sat at the window bottom behind the keyboard. They now ride above it, like the note editor's toolbar.
