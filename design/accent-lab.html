The accent must feel distinct from [[Obsidian]] while staying calm and readable across both themes. See [[Accent Research]] for the full palette study.

◆ Note

Cairn's neutrals are warm-toned. A cold tech-blue clashes; earthy or jewel tones harmonize.

Open decisions to validate against real screens:

Pick a candidate accent trio

Verify --accent-fg passes WCAG AA on buttons

Propagate to mobile/src/theme.ts + splash

Try the token in context: a link, a var(--accent) reference, and an inline #tag all share the accent hue.
